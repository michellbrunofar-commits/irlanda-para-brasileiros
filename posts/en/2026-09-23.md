## 🇮🇪 Ireland

- **Labour calls for Rent Tax Credit to be doubled to €2,000** — The government confirmed it will increase the Rent Tax Credit (currently €1,000 per person) in Budget 2027, though no figure has been set yet; the Labour Party wants it doubled to €2,000. The call comes after rent rose 4.4% in the first quarter of this year alone — the biggest quarterly increase since 2002 — driven by a rule in James Browne's rental reform that allows a landlord to reset a tenancy to market price when the previous tenant leaves and a new one moves in.
