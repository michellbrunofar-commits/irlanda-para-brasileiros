## 🇮🇪 Ireland

- **Ireland sets €32,691 minimum salary for work visas in 4 occupations** — According to the Roadmap for Minimum Annual Remuneration, published by ministers Peter Burke and Alan Dillon, anyone seeking a General Employment Permit as a meat processor, horticulture worker, healthcare assistant, or home carer needs a salary offer of at least €32,691/year. For other occupations under the same visa category, the general threshold rose from €34,000 to €36,605/year, effective March 2026. _[Legit.ng](https://www.legit.ng/people/1732451-ireland-publishes-salary-foreigners-4-occupations-earn-general-employment-permit/) and [Legit.ng](https://www.legit.ng/people/1732442-ireland-increases-annual-salary-requirement-foreigners-seeking-general-employment-permit/), Sep 23, 2026_

- **Housing approvals in Ireland jump 60% in Q2** — 11,912 units were approved between April and June, the highest number in 5 years, up from 7,447 in the same period of 2025. Cork led in the number of homes approved (23.3% of the total), ahead of Dublin (13.2%). _[EchoLive](https://www.echolive.ie/corknews/arid-41914459.html), Sep 23, 2026_

- **Winter fuel allowance set to rise from €38 to €43 a week in Budget 2027** — The Fuel Allowance, paid to more than 470,000 households last winter, starts being paid at the end of September and runs through April. The organization Threshold estimates that 29% of Irish households live in energy poverty, the highest level ever recorded. _[Dublin Live](https://www.dublinlive.ie/news/social-welfare-ireland-scheme-paid-34660381), Sep 23, 2026_

## 💡 What this means for you

- If you work or plan to work as a meat processor, in horticulture, as a healthcare assistant, or as a home carer, check with your employer whether the salary offer meets the €32,691/year threshold — below that, the General Employment Permit won't be approved.
