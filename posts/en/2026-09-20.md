## 🇮🇪 Ireland

- **Major institutional landlord profits €24 million and credits part of the gain to the housing shortage** — A fund linked to French insurer Axa and US investor Kennedy Wilson, owner of 3,506 rental homes in Ireland (including developments like Clancy Quay in Dublin 8 and Capital Dock in the docklands), swung from a €20 million loss to a €24 million profit over the past year, attributing part of the jump to the "significant shortage" of housing in the country.
