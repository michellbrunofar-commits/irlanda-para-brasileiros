## 🇮🇪 Ireland

- **Dublin landlords selling at record pace after rental reform** — Investors selling properties accounted for 24% of all residential sales in Dublin between July and September, up from 20% in the previous quarter and a healthy historical average of just 10% in a normal market, according to a report from estate agency DNG. Two new categories were also created, for Irish graduates and Critical Skills graduates. _[Legit.ng](https://www.legit.ng/people/1732671-ireland-announces-updated-minimum-salary-thresholds-foreign-workers/), 24/09/2026_
